📐 Scoring Methodology

The risk score (0–100) is a transparent, auditable formula — not a black-box ML model. Each signal is weighted by severity, then combined with a multi-signal boost.

final_score = Σ(signal_score × signal_weight) + boost

where:
  boost = min(20, (n_signals − 1) × 5)  // more signals = higher confidence
  clamped to [0, 100]

Signal weights (severity):
  self_is_high_risk = 1.00  (wallet in known flagged registry)
  sanctions_link = 0.95  (tx with OFAC-sanctioned address)
  ransomware_link = 0.90  (tx with known ransomware address)
  darknet_link = 0.85  (tx with darknet marketplace)
  fraud_link = 0.70  (tx with fraud-linked address)
  mixer_interaction = 0.75  (funds to/from mixer/tumbler)
  structuring = 0.50  (many near-identical tx values)
  peel_chain = 0.40  (linear single-hop chain)
  cross_chain_bridge = 0.30  (bridge/swap contract interaction)
  high_velocity = 0.25  (abnormally high tx rate)

Risk thresholds:
  HIGH = score ≥ 60
  MEDIUM = score ≥ 25
  LOW = score < 25
